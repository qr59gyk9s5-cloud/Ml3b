# Incident Response (planned — not yet active)

To be written concretely once there's production traffic to respond to
incidents for (Phase 12+). Placeholder so the structure exists.

## What every incident writeup should eventually capture

What broke, when, who/what noticed it, impact (bookings/payments/data
affected?), root cause, the fix, and the follow-up to prevent recurrence.
Booking-correctness or payment incidents get logged with extra care — see
`docs/security/threat-model.md` for the categories most likely to matter.
